Finders read every passage of your documents and point at the relevant sentences; the app copies them out exactly, and the researcher plans the search and writes the cited answer. The default finder is Qwen3 4B, the best in our benchmark. The default researcher, when downloaded, is the Qwen3.5 9B (Q4_K_M) we benchmarked: smaller researchers such as Qwen3 4B answer less accurately and can be misled by instructions planted in a document. “Same as chat” uses the current model, which avoids swapping models in and out of memory. Parallel finders above Ollama's parallel slots just wait in line.

Paste a Hugging Face GGUF model: hf.co/<user>/<repo>, optionally with :<quantization> (e.g. :Q4_K_M), or the repo's URL. For library tools, pick an instruct model whose page mentions tool or function calling. Models are data, not programs, but anyone can upload one: stick to publishers you trust, and check the model's license on its page.
