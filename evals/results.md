## Pipeline
_Run 2026-11-09 19:38; workers 4, OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL 4, KV cache q8_0_

**rag** = embedding retrieval of the top-k sentences, no finders (the control); **single** = one search for everything; **focused** = the question split into parts, one search per part; **loop** = focused, then follow-up searches that use what earlier parts found; **hybrid** = retrieval of the top-k sentences, widened by 2 sentences either side, and the finders read only those to pick the evidence; **rag-loop** = loop, but each search is a retrieval (no finders, no model swaps). focused and loop search the question itself as well as its parts. Retrieval options: **fused** = embeddings and keywords (BM25) combined by reciprocal rank fusion (default: embeddings only); **context-indexed ±n** = each sentence embedded with n sentences either side; **delivered ±n** = each hit given to the researcher with n sentences either side. For rag-loop, "scans" are retrievals. Finder: Qwen3 4B, 1,500 words per chunk. Correct and clean are over answerable questions; refusals over unanswerable ones.
